# Product requirements: Paperwild

## Intent

Make a child's pencil-page imagination feel inhabited: a hand-wobbled graphite chessboard, uneven colored-pencil shading, informal handwritten notes, and cheerful little doodles on light paper. The woodland cast and living margins should belong to that page. This is a deliberate drawing style; the chess geometry, readable role labels, input targets, motion, audio, and saved games remain dependable.

The active implementation uses `index.html`, `src/loading-screen.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/game.ts`, `src/sketch-scene.ts`, `src/piece-motion.ts`, the character modules, `src/capture-pageant.ts`, `src/field-camp.ts`, and `src/sound.ts`. Online friend play adds `src/online-room-ui.ts`, `src/online-session.ts`, `src/online-sync.ts`, `src/online-types.ts`, and the Convex room functions/schema. The older Three.js renderer is retained source history, not the active runtime.

**Release boundary:** PR #4 is merged into `main` at `3240b0d` and is the published startup release. Invite-based Convex multiplayer is implemented on the new local `feat/convex-multiplayer` branch. It has not been published or deployed to Convex Cloud or the public frontend. The requirements below describe that local implementation; historical TestSprite results do not establish multiplayer acceptance.

## Notebook entry and returning players

- Show the illustrated notebook immediately while the watercolor cast, paper, and fonts load. Its lettering, acorn, and progress ribbon must remain readable with normal motion, OS reduced motion, and a saved manual pause. Asset failures must release loading rather than strand the page.
- With motion enabled, retain the full cover sketch, ready hop, band snap, cover/page opening, and transition into the game on repeat visits too. A warm cache may shorten resource waiting; it must not replace the choreography with an accelerated version.
- Once the loader reveals the page, draw the pencil grid and unfold the residents row by row. Capture intermediate evidence of the visible grid reveal and character pop-up; running these behind the opaque loader does not satisfy the requirement. Selection/status updates must not restart the introduction.
- Provide a keyboard-operable **Skip introduction** control. During loading it requests opening as soon as resources are ready; during opening it skips the remaining cover sequence. Visible pencil drawing and resident pop-up then follow normally, completing before play. Remove the overlay, release inert input, and leave the saved chess position unchanged. Reduced motion uses a readable static entry without cover spins or folded residents.
- Do not begin a restored computer turn until the whole page entry has settled, including after skipping the cover. An unfinished saved game retains its exact history, mode, difficulty, captures, and matching study. A saved game or study that is already over opens a fresh standard game on the next load, keeping valid preferences and mode/difficulty; it must not reopen a terminal study or its result dialog automatically. Preserve the finished history and study separately for explicit revisiting.
- **Pause animations / Resume animations** names the action explicitly. Pausing leaves eyes open, painted parts at rest, and a usable board; resuming restores actual blinking and autonomous movement. A saved manual pause must survive reload independently of the OS setting. Sound remains an independent preference.
- A tab with an active online seat may resume only after notebook entry settles. Opening an invitation pre-fills the online dialog and never silently uses a different tab's saved seat. A fresh tab's saved-seat return requires an explicit action.

## Online friend rooms

Online play is optional. With no `VITE_CONVEX_URL`, computer play, same-device play, stories, preferences, and local saves remain available, and the online dialog explains that its backend is not configured. [CONVEX_SETUP.md](CONVEX_SETUP.md) documents local development, the test layers, and a later cloud release.

| Capability | Implemented behavior | Acceptance requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation | Create a fresh standard game as Amber/White. Copy a link containing its 12-character room code, or enter a friend's code to claim Indigo/Black. | Codes use the shared alphabet and tolerate case, spaces, and separators in the UI. Invalid codes show an actionable error. The first successful independent join takes the second seat; later unrelated joins cannot replace it. No move starts before both seats are taken. |
| Seat authorization | Each browser creates a cryptographically random 256-bit bearer credential. The backend stores its SHA-256 hash and resolves the seat from that hash. | Persist a credential before a create/join attempt so retries keep the same identity. Create/join retries are idempotent. Every query and mutation authenticates a seat; a caller cannot choose its role or read a room using only its code. Credentials and hashes never appear in public snapshots or invitation URLs. |
| Server rules | Convex transactions authorize the credential, require the expected room revision, replay the legal history through chess.js, and validate moves. | Wrong-side, illegal, stale, duplicate, malformed, overlong-history, and expired-room requests leave the room unchanged. Competing joins reserve one seat; competing moves at one revision commit once. Special moves, terminal positions, and repetition use complete history. |
| Reactive presentation | Both browsers subscribe to seat-specific room snapshots with the same shared game, revision, round, and requests. | A local click does not advance an unconfirmed online board. Append only confirmed missing moves through normal animation; equal snapshots do not replay them. Undo, divergence, and a new round safely restore the authoritative position and cancel obsolete effects. Block input until displayed history and animation catch up. |
| Consent | Either seat can request one-ply Undo or a rematch. The opponent accepts or declines; the sender can cancel. | Only the opponent may answer and only the sender may cancel. Decline/cancel preserves the board. A new move clears pending consent, and accepted Undo restores captures/turn/history together. Accepted rematch keeps colors and original expiry. |
| Reconnection | Show this browser's joining, connected, reconnecting, pending, and error states. Reconnect using the same credential. | Do not submit moves or requests while disconnected or busy. Ignore callbacks and mutation completions from a prior room or disposed session. Missing/expired/auth-invalid queries end the apparent connection with a visible error. |
| Seat persistence | Use deployment-scoped per-tab session storage for the active seat and separate local storage for the last explicitly resumable seat. | Reload can resume its own active seat after introduction. A new tab must not claim a host's locally saved seat automatically. Explicit saved-seat return is allowed; the server still determines its role. Storage denial is visible, with no unrecoverable online claim or damage to local chess saves. |
| Room lifetime | A room expires 24 hours after creation. Moves, reconnects, Undo, and rematches do not extend it. Internal hourly cleanup deletes expired rooms in bounded batches. | Every read and mutation rejects an expired room. The client also expires an idle snapshot at `expiresAt` so a quiet subscription cannot remain apparently connected indefinitely. Cleanup deletes at most 100 expired rows per transaction, schedules a continuation only if more remain, preserves live rooms, and has no public endpoint. |
| Connection honesty | A friend seat records that it was claimed; the connection badge reports only this browser's transport. | Do not infer that the opponent is currently online from a taken seat, and do not show simulated presence. There is no lobby, spectator service, chat, or account identity. |

The local game waits while a room is open. Failed room acquisition and **Back to my page** restore the prior local position, study, selection, focus, and result-dismissal context. Online updates must not write the local active/finished-game save keys. Leave clears the active tab credential while preserving a saved seat for explicit return before expiry. Changing local mode, difficulty, or study requires returning to the local page first.

## Little chess stories

Opening a story replaces the current position and uses local mode. Its starting piece is selected. The dialog discloses replacement. **Return to a full game** opens the opponent chooser with **Against the club** selected; opening or canceling it preserves the study. A confirmed computer or same-device choice replaces the study with a standard board. The online choice opens the invitation dialog while keeping the study available for return.

| Story | Interaction | Required result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tea on the sidelines | White pawn e4 captures Black pawn d5. | White acorn at d5; e4 empty; `exd5` in Field notes; one captured Black acorn in the society. Undo restores both pawns and no recorded captures. |
| Room to become | White pawn a7 advances to a8; choose a role. | The chosen role and image replace the pawn. A knight produces an insufficient-material draw; a queen leaves a playable position. Cancel commits nothing. |
| One final move | White queen g6 moves to g7, protected by king f6. | `Qg7#`, White wins, and additional moves are blocked. Undo restores the fox on g6 and an unfinished game. |

## Persistence and export

- Store starting FEN, complete move history, mode, difficulty, and study identifier in `queens-conservatory.game.v1`. The legacy key preserves compatible local games from the earlier prototype.
- Store sound, atmosphere, weather, motion, and the retained compatibility view setting in `queens-conservatory.preferences.v1`. The active board is flat; the view control is hidden.
- Validate and replay saved history. Restore an **unfinished** game so undo and repetition remain correct; a study banner returns only when its starting FEN matches. If the replayed game is **complete** (including a terminal study), preserve its validated game/study in `queens-conservatory.completed-game.v1`, then show a fresh standard position with empty history/captures and no study/result, retaining valid mode/difficulty and preferences. This is page-entry policy; in-session Undo still restores the position before a terminal move.
- **Field kit → Revisit last finished game** appears only when a valid archive exists and the current board is the untouched standard start with no active study. Explicit revisiting restores the completed history, captures, matching study and remembrance without replaying the finale/result automatically. Hide and guard this action once another game has begun or a study is active. If archive storage fails, keep the original completed save rather than overwrite its only copy during automatic startup; normal play stays available, and an explicit new chapter can replace the active save.
- Recover from malformed saves with a usable fresh game. Failed storage writes must not prevent play; show the unavailable-storage state.
- Export PGN as `paperwild-game.pgn`, including the Paperwild event, player headers, moves, and setup FEN where needed. Export is disabled before a move.

Local games and preferences remain in the same browser and origin. Online room history lives in its configured Convex deployment; it does not synchronize offline games, studies, preferences, or a personal account archive. Seat recovery depends on the private browser credential, and clearing that storage can lose access. Room invitations share the game code without exposing that credential.

## Sound and movement lifecycle

- The rules engine commits a legal move once. Its finite movement/capture sequence then owns the board's busy state; selection/status rerenders must not restart or truncate it.
- Movement plans provide contact times to the sound score. Launch/contact and arrival sounds must follow the same choreography, rather than unrelated timers.
- Promotion remains uncommitted until the choice is made. A settled promotion, check, or terminal move receives its appropriate feedback once; render and reload are silent state reconstruction.
- Undo, restart, mode changes, and story replacement invalidate pending feedback as well as obsolete computer work. Intentional cancellation must settle the displayed board and release waiting tasks.
- Online append updates wait for the current animation before presenting the next confirmed move. Incoming rewind or rematch replaces the board immediately and invalidates the old animation's completion. Reactive echoes and mutation acknowledgements must not duplicate moves, sounds, or ceremonies.
- Sound uses browser-generated sources, a bounded voice pool, and a master mute. It needs no external audio assets or backend. A refused resume or unsupported audio must leave chess usable without an uncaught rejection.
- An enabled saved preference does not authorize page-load autoplay. After hiding, sound requires a fresh trusted interaction and must not replay stale cues.
- Reduced motion and sound are independent preferences. A static board can still provide concise feedback after the player opts into sound.

## Quality and evidence

1. Visible characters, square labels, history, turn, captures, and the rules engine must describe the same position.
2. Undo, restart, study selection, and mode/difficulty changes must safely cancel obsolete computer work.
3. Motion must stay decorative: reduced motion, image loading failure, hidden-page transitions, and resize must preserve play.
4. Test assertions must check complete feature outcomes. A screenshot, nonempty URL, or successful button click alone does not prove correct chess.
5. Build and strict frontend/backend type checking must succeed. Browser unit tests, isolated Convex transaction tests, and local live WebSocket tests provide separate evidence before an online release.
